01 About

I work where machine learning meets hardware: compilers, high-level synthesis and inference on constrained devices — FPGAs, microcontrollers and GPUs.

My MSc thesis is on FPGA acceleration of LLM inference, supervised by Prof. Fabrizio Ferrandi, in collaboration with CERN as part of the Edge SpAIce project.

I'm looking for roles in edge AI, ML compilers and inference hardware, and I'm open to relocation. Based in Milan, Italy.

02 Projects

StreaMatrix

PyTorch → FPGA compilation flow

  • MLIR
  • LLVM
  • PandA-Bambu
  • MLIR pipeline based on Stream-HLS that lowers PyTorch models to LLVM IR and uses PandA-Bambu as the synthesis backend for hls4ml, generating Verilog for Xilinx UltraScale+ (xcu250).
  • Custom MLIR passes for linalg.matmul: tiling, parallelisation of vector reductions, fusion of tiles into parallel llvm.matrix.multiply calls, and load hoisting.
  • Load/compute/store restructuring took an 8×8 GEMM kernel from 93 to 27 FSM states, with 0 conflicts and 0 BRAM. Fixed a false conflict in Bambu's module binding and built a design-space exploration flow over parallelism parameters.

GPU sequence-to-graph alignment

Politecnico di Milano · with GenoGra

  • CUDA
  • CUDA acceleration of Theseus, an affine-gap sequence-to-graph aligner (an extension of WFA) for pangenomics.
  • Distributed the kernel's serial phases across the thread block: up to 3.07× speedup at 256 threads.
  • Profiled down to the remaining bottleneck: DRAM bandwidth.
