Biography

Pietro Benecchi is an MSc student in Computer Science and Engineering at Politecnico di Milano, with graduation expected in December 2026. The focus of the work is where machine learning meets hardware: compilers, high-level synthesis, and inference on constrained devices such as FPGAs, microcontrollers, and GPUs.

The MSc thesis, supervised by Prof. Fabrizio Ferrandi and carried out in collaboration with CERN within the Edge SpAIce project, focuses on FPGA acceleration of LLM inference, with contributions to hls4ml and PandA-Bambu. Pietro is co-author of a paper at SEAMS that received the Distinguished Paper Award, and of a poster at the 3rd FPGA Developers' Forum at CERN.

Pietro is looking for roles in edge AI, ML compilers, and inference hardware, is open to relocation, and is based in Milan, Italy.

Projects

StreaMatrix — PyTorch → FPGA compilation flow

MLIR · LLVM · PandA-Bambu

MLIR pipeline based on Stream-HLS that lowers PyTorch models to LLVM IR and uses PandA-Bambu as the synthesis backend for hls4ml, generating Verilog for Xilinx UltraScale+ (xcu250).

Highlights:

  • Custom MLIR passes for linalg.matmul: tiling, parallelisation of vector reductions, fusion of tiles into parallel llvm.matrix.multiply calls, load hoisting
  • Load/compute/store restructuring brought an 8×8 GEMM kernel from 93 to 27 FSM states, with 0 conflicts and 0 BRAM
  • Fixed a false conflict in Bambu's module binding; design-space exploration flow over parallelism parameters

GPU sequence-to-graph alignment

CUDA · Politecnico di Milano, with GenoGra

CUDA acceleration of Theseus, an affine-gap sequence-to-graph aligner (an extension of WFA) for pangenomics.

Highlights:

  • Distribution of the kernel's serial phases across the thread block: up to 3.07× speedup at 256 threads
  • Profiling down to the remaining bottleneck (DRAM bandwidth)
